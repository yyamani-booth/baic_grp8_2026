# Liaison: 5 test cases for itinerary generation (draft, edit as a group)

Setup for all: trip text = the Italy example unless stated. Profiles are entered in Step 3.

| # | Type | Input | What a good output looks like | Why it matters |
|---|------|-------|-------------------------------|----------------|
| 1 | Typical | Italy example (Venice/Florence/Naples, 10 days, early Feb, <$4,000 pp). Profiles: organizer (food markets, art museums, "no allergies", walks a lot), Dad ("no pork or red meat, no alcohol, can walk ~30 min before rest"), Sister (cultural, nature, no nightlife, no pork/alcohol). | Exactly 10 days x 3 items in Venice -> Florence -> Naples order. Every meal avoids pork, red meat and alcohol as a primary offering. No walking tour longer than ~30 min without a seated option. Each traveler has at least 2 items matching their interests (organizer: market/museum; sister: nature/history). Day 1 arrival, day 10 departure. | Core happy path from the storyboard. |
| 2 | Typical | Weekend trip: "4 days in Lisbon, 2 couples in their 60s and 30s, budget $1,500 each, late May". Profiles: one vegetarian, one with a bad knee ("no stairs or steep hills"), two with only interests (fado, tile museums, beaches). | 4 days covered; vegetarian-friendly meals throughout; no steep-hill walking (Alfama hill climbs avoided or replaced by tram/taxi); budget-aware (no luxury items). | Checks it generalizes beyond the hard-coded Italy case. |
| 3 | Edge | Italy trip, but one traveler writes only "I'm fine with anything" (profile mostly "not specified"), another wrote "I don't have any allergies" (allergies shows "not specified"), and the trip text omits dates. | Still returns a full itinerary; does not invent dates; does not treat "not specified" as "safe" (adds e.g. "ask venue about allergens"); not dominated by the one traveler who gave detail. | Under-specified intent is our #2 risk (Assignment 4). |
| 4 | Failure | Italy trip; Dad's profile says "I can walk maybe 30 minutes before I need to rest"; another traveler loves hiking ("I love long hikes and walking tours, 3+ hours"). | No 3-hour walking tour or steep hike in any shared item; the hiker's interest is served with a short or seated alternative, or a clearly optional split. Failure to watch for: the simulated sample's 3-hour Cannaregio walking tour appearing. | Mobility mismatch = costliest error (Assignment 3/4), plus fairness for the minority traveler. |
| 5 | Failure | Italy trip; one traveler is "allergic to shellfish and peanuts" and "no pork"; another "vegan"; a third "halal"; organizer wants "seafood in Venice, it's the whole point". | Every meal compatible with ALL stated constraints simultaneously (no shellfish/peanuts/pork; vegan-compatible options; halal). Does not recommend a "seafood dinner" without flagging the shellfish allergy conflict. Never claims a venue is "allergy-safe" as a guarantee. | Hard-constraint conflict and over-reliance on fluent plans (Assignment 4 verification risk). |
